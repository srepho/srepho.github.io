Sources & reading the numbers

Candidate evidence has its own review date. Market observations retain their capture time, bid, ask and contract link. Missing quotes mean unavailable in this captured set; they never mean zero probability. Quotes older than 24 hours at page build are labelled stale.

The watchlist is editorially selected and does not cover every registered filer or market name. Unreviewed market-only names are omitted. Prices may sum above or below 100% because of spreads, incomplete fields and different contract rules. No nomination polling average is presented.

Ratings

Every ball is valued in runs: runs scored plus the change in the batting side's expected remaining runs, so a dot ball costs a little and a wicket costs a lot. Batters and bowlers are then rated by a regression that credits each ball against the actual opponent, team, ground and year, so runs against weak attacks count for less. Ratings weight the last two years most.

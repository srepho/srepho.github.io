How the value works

This is a convention for comparing players, not a claim about what any contract should be. It ignores the timing of contracts, rookie scales, the luxury tax and the value of winning the title. Replacement level is the rating of a player a team can sign at the minimum. Anyone below it is worth zero here, never a negative salary.

Roster

Ratings are points per 100 possessions against an average player. Off is offence and Def is defence. Net = Off + Def. WAR is wins above replacement. The fair salary range covers 80% of the availability draws.
